# Teaching with Historical Maps at the David Rumsey Map Center
 
My paper on our teaching practices and methodologies has been published online -- at long last! -- in a special issue of the open access journal *Nuevo Mundos, Mundos Nuevos* edited by Amaia Cabranes Rubio and Jordana Dym. 

It's available in French, English, and Spanish at this link: [Scholarship and Serendipity: Teaching with Historical Maps at the David Rumsey Map Center
](https://journals.openedition.org/nuevomundo/110290) 

The rest of the issue, entitled "Cartes et enseignement aux/des Amériques : passé et présent d’une pratique pédagogique / *Maps and teaching in the Americas: past and present of a pedagogical practice*" can be accessed [here](https://journals.openedition.org/nuevomundo/105270).
